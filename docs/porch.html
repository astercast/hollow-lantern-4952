How the fees move

Why a porch

$PORCH isn't a trading coin — you win by sitting. Coins sit in the pools, the town trades around them, fees come to you. The active coin runs; $PORCH is the machine underneath it — deeper liquidity, cheaper trading, holders paid over time.

The pools

  • $MDOG/$MUSEBOOK — the foundation. Full-range, live. Where $MDOG gets its price.
  • $PORCH/$MUSEBOOK — the front door. Seeded with 85B $PORCH. Every trade pays 1.75%: 0.665% to the Muse Dogs treasury in $MUSEBOOK, 0.285% auto-compounded back into the pool as locked liquidity, the rest to LPs.
  • $PORCH/$MDOG — the bridge. Ties the two community coins together so every rotation pays a fee here.

Where APR comes from

  • Trading fees. Every swap pays the pool. Deeper pools mean less slippage, less slippage attracts traders, more traders means more fees. That loop is the engine.
  • Incentives. 15B $PORCH vests to the locked launch wallet over one year (30-day cliff). Half of it is earmarked for Merkl incentive campaigns on the pools — LPs earn fees plus $PORCH. Campaigns launch when announced.

The flywheel, step by step

  1. $PORCH launches; the $PORCH/$MUSEBOOK pool opens with deep seeded liquidity.
  2. The $PORCH/$MDOG bridge pool opens.
  3. Vested $PORCH flows into Merkl campaigns (planned — after the vesting cliff) — LPs earn fees plus $PORCH rewards.
  4. High APRs attract more LPs → deeper pools → less slippage → the best place to trade these coins.
  5. More volume → more swap fees to LPs, more creator fees to the treasury in $MUSEBOOK.
  6. Treasury grows → funds the next round of incentives and holder rewards → repeat.

What it means for you

  • Hold a Muse Dog: once the contracts launch, the weekly ETH rewards vault gains a second income stream — $PORCH creator fees flowing into the treasury in $MUSEBOOK. 40% of the vested $PORCH funds an on-site claim engine: hold the dog, claim $PORCH directly. Planned — opens when the NFT contracts deploy.
  • LP: earn swap fees plus Merkl $PORCH incentives — Merkl can boost NFT holders, so loyalty pays more.
  • Trade: deeper pools mean less slippage on every $MDOG or $PORCH trade.
  • Watch: the treasury, the pools, and the Merkl campaigns are all on-chain. Every claim on this page is auditable.
