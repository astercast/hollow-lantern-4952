Holder rewards

Holding a Muse Dog pays three ways: ETH from resale royalties, PORCH from vesting, MUSEBOOK from PORCH creator fees. All three are claimed here, pro-rata by the dogs you hold.

🟢 The weekly MUSEBOOK leaderboard is live now — see the board. NFT royalty claims launch with the contracts.

Weekly MUSEBOOK leaderboard — live now

PORCH trading fees are claimed in MUSEBOOK and flow into the treasury. Every Monday, 1/8 of the treasury's MUSEBOOK balance becomes that week's reward pot — never the whole thing, so it keeps going. The figure on the board reads live from chain.

  • 50% to PORCH holders
  • 30% to MDOG holders
  • 20% stays in the treasury for future use

Scores are time-weighted — how much you hold and how long you hold it. Every holder shows up under a dog codename; no addresses on the board, ever. Only wallets linked to a verified musebook identity earn. Rewards are claimed, never airdropped; unclaimed rolls into the next epoch.

Separate from the future Merkl rewards

The weekly MUSEBOOK leaderboard above is not the Merkl program — they are two different systems:

  • Weekly MUSEBOOK leaderboard (live now): funded by PORCH creator fees, paid in MUSEBOOK, split 50/30/20.
  • Future Merkl rewards (coming later): funded by vested PORCH — 50% of the vested supply goes to Merkl LP incentives, 40% funds an on-site claim engine for Muse Dogs NFT holders, 10% to Mikey. Nothing unlocks before the 30-day vesting cliff (about October 23, 2026).

When the Merkl campaigns go live, they'll be announced separately — nothing here changes when they do.

Where the weekly ETH comes from

Every Muse Dog resale carries a 7% royalty, split four ways:

  • 10% to Mikey
  • 50% to the weekly holder pot — claimed on this page
  • 20% buys MDOG + musebook for the MDOG/MUSEBOOK pool
  • 20% buys MDOG for the MDOG/ETH pool

Both LP positions mint straight to a dead address — locked forever.

Three ways to get paid

  • ETH — 50% of every resale royalty, weekly, pro-rata.
  • PORCH — 40% of the vested PORCH supply funds an on-site claim engine for NFT holders.
  • MUSEBOOK — PORCH creator fees are claimed in MUSEBOOK and stack in the treasury for holder rewards.
