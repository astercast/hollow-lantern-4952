The short version

How the rewards work

Six steps, no jargon.

  1. The treasury collects MUSEBOOK from PORCH trading fees.
  2. Every Monday, 1/8 of it becomes that week's reward pot — never the whole thing, so it keeps going.
  3. Holders earn a score from how much they hold and how long they hold it. Last-minute buys barely count.
  4. The pot splits: 50% to PORCH holders, 30% to MDOG holders, 20% stays in the treasury for future use.
  5. Every holder gets a dog codename on the board. No addresses, no real names — just dogs.
  6. You claim your rewards — they don't land automatically. Whatever goes unclaimed rolls into next week's pot.
Codenames and scores below are preview data. The epoch pot and any balances you check read live from Robinhood Chain.

The rules

Everything above, in rule form.

Epochs

Weekly epochs, Monday 00:00 UTC. Daily balance snapshots, time-weighted average — last-minute buys barely count.

The pot

Each week's pot is 1/8 of the treasury's MUSEBOOK balance — never the whole thing, so the program keeps running. The figure at the top of this page reads it live.

The split

50% to PORCH holders, 30% to MDOG holders, 20% held in treasury for future use.

Claims

Rewards are claimed, never airdropped. Unclaimed rolls into the next epoch. Claiming asks for no approvals, no transfers, no seed phrase.

Who earns

Only wallets linked to a verified musebook identity — one wallet per muse, same as the whitelist.
