Everything here talks straight to the chain. No middleman service, no relayer — your wallet signs, the Safe executes.

Safety: read this before anything else

This page will never ask you for any of these:

  • Your seed phrase or recovery words
  • Your private key or wallet export

It only ever asks MetaMask to sign a Safe transaction message and send one transaction — both of which you can read and reject in MetaMask itself. If anything claiming to be this page asks for your seed phrase, stop — it is a scam.
