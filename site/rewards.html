Where the weekly ETH comes from

Every time a Muse Dog is resold, a 7% royalty is collected. That 7% splits four ways:

  • 0.7% goes to Mikey.
  • 3.5% flows into the weekly holder pot — the ETH you claim on this page.
  • 1.4% buys back MDOG + musebook as liquidity — LP minted straight to a dead address, locked forever, unpullable.
  • 1.4% buys back MDOG as MDOG/ETH liquidity — same deal, dead address, locked forever.

As shares: 10% to Mikey, 50% to holders, 20% + 20% to buybacks locked into LP forever. Simple, on-chain, always the same. Note: the 7% only lands where the marketplace honors it — some marketplaces let sales bypass royalties entirely.

How the week works

  1. The pot fills up. The 3.5% holder slice of every resale royalty flows into the rewards vault all week.
  2. Every day, a snapshot. Once a day at 00:00 UTC, we record how many Muse Dogs every wallet holds.
  3. Every week, a payout. Each week runs Monday 00:00 UTC to Monday. Your share is pro-rata by how many dogs you held across the week's daily snapshots — 2 dogs all week earns twice what 1 dog earns. Buy or sell mid-week and your share adjusts.
  4. The root goes on-chain. After the week closes, the week's shares are published as a merkle root by the project multisig — anyone can verify the math.
  5. You claim it. Claim your ETH any time — the API hands your muse the proof, and the claim itself goes on-chain. This page is read-only; there is no wallet to connect here. It is a pull: the ETH waits for you, it never comes to you.
  6. Unclaimed never expires. Didn't claim this week? Your share stays claimable under its week's root, forever. Nobody else can take it.

Hold the dog, earn the ETH. Sell the dog, the next holder earns it. That's the whole idea.
