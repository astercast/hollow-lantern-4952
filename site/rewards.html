Where the weekly ETH comes from

Every time a Muse Dog is resold, a 7% royalty is collected. That 7% splits four ways:

  • 0.7% goes to Mikey.
  • 3.5% flows into the weekly holder pot — the ETH you claim on this page.
  • 1.4% buys back MDOG + musebook as liquidity — LP minted straight to a dead address, locked forever, unpullable.
  • 1.4% buys back MDOG as MDOG/ETH liquidity — same deal, dead address, locked forever.

As shares: 10% to Mikey, 50% to holders, 20% + 20% to buybacks locked into LP forever. Simple, on-chain, always the same. Note: the 7% only lands where the marketplace honors it — some marketplaces let sales bypass royalties entirely.

ETH + PORCH + MUSEBOOK — the full holder picture

ETH is only one third of it. Holding a Muse Dog pays three ways — and the other two run through the porch page, the Pools of Rare Coin Holdings engine:

  • ETH — from NFT royalties. 3.5% of every Muse Dog resale royalty flows into the weekly pot on this page, claimable pro-rata. The plan — not live until the contracts deploy.
  • PORCH — from vesting. PORCH comes from the vesting allocation: 40% of the 15 billion vested PORCH is earmarked for a planned on-site claim engine that lets NFT holders claim PORCH directly.
  • MUSEBOOK — from PORCH creator trading fees. Every PORCH creator trading fee is claimed in MUSEBOOK and swept straight to the Muse Dogs treasury, stacking there for phase-1 holder rewards.

Check out the porch page to see how the pools, the fees, and the holder rewards fit together — pools where the town's coins sit, earn fees on every trade, and compound.
