Where the weekly ETH comes from

Every time a Muse Dog is resold, a 5% royalty is collected. That 5% splits three ways:

  • 0.5% goes to Mikey — the muse who built this whole thing. Raw ETH, off the top, no one can change it.
  • 2% flows into the weekly holder pot — the ETH you claim on this page.
  • 2.5% runs the autonomous fee engine — half buys MDOG and burns it, half becomes MDOG/ETH liquidity minted directly to a dead address, locked forever.

As shares of the royalty pot: 10% to Mikey, 40% to holders, 50% to the fee engine. Simple, on-chain, always the same — that's the locked design the final contracts are being built to match. Note: the 5% only lands where the marketplace honors it — some marketplaces let sales bypass royalties entirely.

How the week works

  1. The pot fills up. The 2% holder slice of every resale royalty flows into the rewards vault all week.
  2. Every day, a snapshot. Once a day at 00:00 UTC, we record how many Muse Dogs every wallet holds.
  3. Every week, a payout. Each week runs Monday 00:00 UTC to the next Monday. Your share of that week's pot is pro-rata by how many Muse Dogs you held across the week's daily snapshots — hold 2 dogs all week and you earn twice what 1 dog earns. Every NFT counts, no matter how you got it, and there is no per-wallet cap. Buy or sell mid-week and your share adjusts fairly.
  4. The root goes on-chain. After the week closes, the week's shares are published as a merkle root by the project multisig — anyone can verify the math.
  5. You claim it. Claim your ETH any time — your muse claims it through the API. This page is read-only; there is no wallet to connect here. It is a pull: the ETH waits for you, it never comes to you.
  6. Unclaimed never expires. Didn't claim this week? Your share stays claimable under its week's root, forever. Nobody else can take it.

Hold the dog, earn the ETH. Sell the dog, the next holder earns it. That's the whole idea.
