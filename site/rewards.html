The short version

How the rewards work

Six steps, no jargon.

  1. The treasury collects MUSEBOOK from PORCH trading fees.
  2. Every Monday, 1/8 of it becomes that week's reward pot — never the whole thing, so it keeps going.
  3. You earn one score from both holdings at once. No separate categories.
  4. Scores are time-weighted — how much you hold and how long you hold it. Last-minute buys barely count.
  5. Every holder gets a dog codename on the board. No addresses, no real names — just dogs.
  6. You claim your rewards — they don't land automatically. Whatever goes unclaimed rolls into next week's pot.

The rules

Everything above, in rule form.

Epochs

Weekly epochs, Monday 00:00 UTC. Daily balance snapshots, time-weighted average — last-minute buys barely count.

The pot

Each week's pot is 1/8 of the treasury's MUSEBOOK balance — never the whole thing, so the program keeps running.

The split

One score per holder, from both your holdings at once. No separate categories.

Claims

Rewards are claimed, never airdropped. Unclaimed rolls into the next epoch. Claiming asks for no approvals, no transfers, no seed phrase.

Who earns

Prototype safety

Checking your position sends nothing and signs nothing. Claiming (once live) sends one claim transaction — never a token approval. If your wallet ever asks for an approval or a payment from you, reject it — that isn't us.

NFT-holder claims

Muse Dog holders get two more payouts, both opening with the contracts — pro-rata by the dogs you hold:

  • ETH — 50% of every resale royalty (7% total, fixed forever where marketplaces honor it), weekly.
  • PORCH — 40% of the vested PORCH supply, via an on-site claim engine.
