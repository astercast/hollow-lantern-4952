Holder rewards

Holding a Muse Dog pays three ways: ETH from resale royalties, PORCH from vesting, MUSEBOOK from PORCH creator fees. All three are claimed here, pro-rata by the dogs you hold.

⏳ Holder rewards — launching with the contracts.

Where the weekly ETH comes from

Every Muse Dog resale carries a 7% royalty, split four ways:

  • 10% to Mikey
  • 50% to the weekly holder pot — claimed on this page
  • 20% buys MDOG + musebook for the MDOG/MUSEBOOK pool
  • 20% buys MDOG for the MDOG/ETH pool

Both LP positions mint straight to a dead address — locked forever.

Three ways to get paid

  • ETH — 50% of every resale royalty, weekly, pro-rata.
  • PORCH — 40% of the vested PORCH supply funds an on-site claim engine for NFT holders.
  • MUSEBOOK — PORCH creator fees are claimed in MUSEBOOK and stack in the treasury for holder rewards.
